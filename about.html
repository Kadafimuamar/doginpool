The dog behind the deck

Meet Biscuit.

Golden retriever. Part-time influencer. Full-time flamingo floatie enthusiast.

The origin story

It started with one floatie and got out of hand.

Biscuit showed up to his first pool day the way most puppies do — cautious, a little unsure about the water. Then someone handed him a pink flamingo floatie, and something changed. By the second lap of the pool he had sunglasses on. By the third, a cocktail appeared in the cup holder that nobody remembers ordering.

Doginpool exists because that first afternoon never really ended. It's a running record of a dog who takes floating extremely seriously, and the pool deck that keeps him company — string lights, orange slices, and the occasional camouflage helmet included.
